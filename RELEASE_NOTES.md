# 🏭 Homestead: Steampunk Voxel City Builder

## Release Notes — Version 0.1.0 "First Steam"

---

### 🎮 About This Game

**Homestead** is a first-person steampunk voxel city builder where you construct an industrial revolution-era city block by block. Place brass walls, lay copper pipes, fire up steam boilers, and watch your Victorian metropolis come to life — all in a fully procedural voxel world.

Built with **Godot 4.7**, Homestead combines the creative freedom of voxel building with the strategic depth of city management, wrapped in a rich steampunk aesthetic of brass, copper, iron, and steam.

---

### ✨ What's New in 0.1.0

#### 🌍 A Living, Breathing World
Step into a procedurally generated world with **four distinct biomes**:
- **Flatlands** — Lush green grasslands, perfect for your first buildings
- **Hills** — Rugged stone mountains hiding valuable coal deposits
- **River Valleys** — Winding waterways cutting through the landscape
- **Industrial Zones** — Flat cobblestone plateaus dotted with iron beams and decorative gears

Every world is unique. Explore, find the perfect spot, and start building.

#### 🔨 Build Anything, Block by Block
Choose from **26 different voxel types** — from basic dirt and stone to brass walls, copper pipes, iron beams, glass panels, and glowing gas lamps. Place them freely in voxel mode, or use **22 pre-designed prefabs** to stamp down complete buildings instantly.

#### 🏗️ 22 Steampunk Buildings Across 5 Categories

| Category | Buildings |
|----------|-----------|
| 🏠 **Residential** | Worker House · Brass Apartment · Victorian Mansion · Tenement Block |
| ⚙️ **Industrial** | Steam Boiler · Foundry · Factory · Coal Mine |
| 🛒 **Commercial** | Market Stall · Shop · Tavern |
| 🔧 **Infrastructure** | Pump Station · Water Treatment · Reservoir · Steam Generator · Power Substation · Sewer Outlet |
| 🎨 **Decorative** | Gas Lamp · Gear Fountain · Clock Tower · Bronze Statue · Park Bench |

Each building has **connection points** for water, steam, power, and waste networks — plan your infrastructure carefully!

#### 💧 Simulation Systems Under the Hood
Behind the brass and gears, a full simulation engine runs:
- **Water Network** — Pressure-based flow through connected pipes
- **Power Grid** — Steam generators convert heat to electricity
- **Waste Management** — Collection and treatment keep your city clean
- **Citizen Satisfaction** — Happy citizens mean a thriving city

Adjust the **time scale** from 0.1× to 10× to watch your city evolve at your pace.

#### 🎨 Handcrafted Steampunk Visuals
Every voxel type has its own **procedurally generated texture** — brass plates with rivets, weathered stone with cracks, wooden planks with grain, copper pipes with flanges, glowing coal embers, and rippling water. Face-based lighting gives depth to every structure without heavy shaders.

#### 💾 Save Your Progress
Your city auto-saves every 5 minutes. Create named save slots and return to your creation anytime.

### 🖥️ System Requirements

- **OS:** Windows 10/11 (64-bit)
- **GPU:** Vulkan-compatible graphics card
- **RAM:** 4 GB minimum
- **Storage:** 200 MB
- **Engine:** Godot 4.7.2 (Forward Plus renderer)

---

### 📋 How to Run

1. Download and extract the release archive
2. Run `Homestead.exe` from the `build/` folder
3. Click into the game window to capture your mouse
4. Press **B** to open the build menu and start creating!

> **Note:** If running from the Godot editor, open `project.godot` and press F5.
